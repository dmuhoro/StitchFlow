# Roadmap Brief

> Canonical: `docs/roadmap.md`.

## Now — v1.0.0 launch readiness

Reproducible schema, proven RLS, bundled assets, wired persistence, CI, release tag, clean tree,
honest docs. Deployment is the only human-owned gate.

## Next — pilot with one atelier

- Owner onboarding wizard: create atelier, seed lookbook, invite artisans.
- Server-side appointment capacity and double-booking protection.
- Authenticated end-to-end test with three real identities (owner, artisan, patron).
- Media storage for garment photography with signed URLs.
- Email/WhatsApp transactional notifications.

## Later — business surface

- Quotes, invoices, deposits, and payment reconciliation (M-Pesa, card).
- Fabric inventory, QC/rework, delivery and alteration history.
- Measurement versioning and garment specification snapshots.
- Consent, retention, export and deletion workflows.
- Backups with a restore drill, and operational monitoring/alerting.

## Explicitly out of scope for v1.0.0

- Payments.
- Multi-atelier membership per user.
- Native mobile apps.
- Generic fashion-commerce catalog features.

## Completion rule

A roadmap item moves to "Now" only when its acceptance test exists and can be run. Otherwise it is
aspirational and must be labelled as such.
