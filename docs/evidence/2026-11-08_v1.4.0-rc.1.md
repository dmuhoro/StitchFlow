`https://<ref>.supabase.co` URL and publishable key.

## Negative proofs

1. **Appointment insert bypass.** Before `20261107220000_restrict_client_appointment_insert.sql`, a
   client-role insert of a `confirmed` appointment with no linked booking succeeded on a disposable
   Postgres 16 replay of the tracked chain. After the migration, RLS tests 13–14 and staff S7a–S7d
   refuse a confirmed or staff-assigned insert while still accepting an own-tenant pending request.
2. **Demo artifact in production.** A demo-mode build in a production context fails `verify:build`,
   so a missing or overridden `VITE_DATA_MODE` cannot ship the demo PIN and identities.
